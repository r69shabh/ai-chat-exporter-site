AI Chat Exporter Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

Conversation data

The extension reads messages, conversation titles and links from supported ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini pages you use. This data is processed on your device to make the export you request; the extension does not transmit your conversations to us or any third party. Bulk export opens the conversations you choose on the same website in background tabs. The website may receive its normal requests to load those pages.

When preparing a PDF, the data is held temporarily in the browser’s extension session storage and cleared as the preview opens. If it cannot open, the temporary entry expires for use after one minute and is removed during a subsequent PDF export or when the browser ends the extension session. Downloaded files stay on your device until you delete or share them. Optional metadata in a Pro export can include the conversation URL and any available timestamps.

Pro license verification

When you activate an optional Pro license, AI Chat Exporter sends the key, its public product ID, and a flag to avoid incrementing the use counter to Gumroad over HTTPS. The request exposes standard network information such as your IP address to Gumroad. The extension saves the key and the last successful verification time in local browser extension storage and rechecks the license when it next needs it after seven days. There is a further seven-day grace period if Gumroad cannot be reached.

Gumroad handles purchases and payment information according to its privacy policy. The extension does not collect or store payment information. Gumroad’s verification response is used only to check if the license remains valid; the extension does not save purchase or receipt details.

Your choices

Free exports do not require an account with us or a license key. In Firefox, you can decline or revoke the optional permission for sending the license key. To remove a saved key, open the extension’s Pro license section and click “Remove license from this device,” or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes extension storage, but does not delete your exported files.

Tracking and contact

The extension includes no analytics, advertising, telemetry or tracking code. For privacy questions, contact the AI Chat Exporter publisher through the support contact shown in the store listing.
